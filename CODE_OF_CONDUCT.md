# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project exists to be used and trusted with evidence about text. That only
works if the people involved can disagree without being punished for it, and if
nobody is pressured into a conclusion the evidence does not support.

## Expected behaviour

- Disagree with the argument, not the person.
- Say "I don't know" when that is the truth, and treat a withheld label as a
  legitimate answer rather than a failure to finish.
- Take people's claims about their data seriously, and do not ask for more of
  it than the question needs.
- Be accurate about what a measurement does and does not show. Overclaiming a
  number is treated as a bug here, not a style issue.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Pressure to report a detector result the evidence does not support, or to
  suppress one that it does.
- Misrepresenting the provenance of a result, or of the data behind it.

## Scope

This applies in issues, discussions, and anywhere someone is
representing this project.

## Reporting

Report privately to marcin@marcinwolny.net, or through
GitHub's reporting tools. Reports are handled confidentially, and the reporter
is told what happened.

All reports are reviewed. Maintainers who do not follow this code of conduct
in good faith may face temporary or permanent consequences as appropriate.
